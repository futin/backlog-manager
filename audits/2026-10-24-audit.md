Important | security | skills/backlog-tick/tools/tick.mjs:571 | The scheduled backlog-tick takes the same outsider-filed path with no operator present: it picks any open tracker bug or task with `groomed === true` that is not marked planReview or inReview, and runs execute on it to a PR. **Failure scenario.** A dashboard job runs `/backlog-tick` on a schedule against a tracker project whose repo is public, which invariants.md allows. **Verification.** UPHELD — At tick.mjs:571, trackerExecuteCandidate drops an item only when `groomed !== true`, `planReview === true` or `inReview === true`. `groomed` comes from `deriveGroomed(section, issue.body)` (map-issue.ts:232), and `section` comes from the `type:*` label. So an outsider who fills `## Plan` produces an open, groomed task with no plan-review label. Nothing in the tracker, item sources, tick or execute code checks the issue author or author_association, and decide() (tick.mjs:436-439) passes the pick straight to backlog-execute `--finish pr`.
