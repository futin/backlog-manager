Refund ledger entries

Every refund, partial or full, writes a ledger entry against the original capture so finance can reconcile without reading Stripe events.

  • One entry per refund attempt, including failures, with the gateway id.
  • Entries are append-only; a reversal is a new entry with kind: reversal.
  • The nightly report reads the ledger, never the events table.

Out of scope: multi-currency refunds (PAY-151 tracks the rounding bug first).
